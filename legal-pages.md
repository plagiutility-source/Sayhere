## Payment Policy (Draft)

1. **Billing**: Paid plans are billed monthly in advance and renew
   automatically until cancelled.

2. **Non-Refundable**: Payments are non-refundable once a billing cycle has
   begun, except where required by applicable law.

3. **Failed Payments**: If a recurring payment fails, a 3-day grace period
   applies. Failure to resolve payment within this window will result in
   service suspension. We are not liable for any business impact resulting
   from suspension due to non-payment.

4. **Upgrades**: Take effect immediately; the new plan's charge (prorated or
   full, per your billing engine's logic) is billed at time of upgrade.

5. **Downgrades**: Take effect at the end of the current billing cycle; no
   partial refund is issued for the unused portion of the higher plan.

6. **Currency & Taxes**: Indian customers are billed in ₹ via Cashfree.
   Paid-plan billing for international customers is not yet available; they
   may sign up and use the Free tier only until a second payment provider
   (e.g. Stripe) is added for global/USD billing. Applicable taxes (GST, VAT,
   sales tax) will be added where legally required once international billing
   is live.

## Cancellation Policy (Draft)

1. Cancellation requests must be submitted through the in-app cancellation
   form, including a reason for cancellation.

2. Submitting a cancellation request does not immediately terminate the
   account. Our team will review and reach out within 48 hours. Once the
   cancellation is confirmed (or, if it is still undecided 2 days before your
   renewal date, automatically), no further charges are made.

3. Service and access continue uninterrupted through the end of the current
   paid billing cycle, regardless of when the request was submitted. After
   that, your account moves to the Free plan; it is not closed or deleted.

4. No partial-month or pro-rated refunds are issued for cancellation
   mid-cycle.

5. When a paid plan ends, your account moves to the Free plan and your data
   (feedback history, analytics, branding) is kept. Any additional locations
   beyond the Free plan's limit are switched off and kept for a limited period
   (currently {RETENTION_DAYS_AFTER_CANCEL} days) in case you upgrade again, after
   which they and their data are permanently deleted, in line with our Privacy
   Policy. You may delete your whole account at any time.

6. Refunds are not automatic. Any refund is issued at our discretion or where
   required by law, and only by our team after review.
